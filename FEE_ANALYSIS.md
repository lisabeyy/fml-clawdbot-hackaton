# Fee Analysis & Comparison

## Current Fee Structure

### Our Model
```
When a trader bets 100 SOL:
- Creator fee: 2 SOL (2%)
- Into trading pool: 98 SOL (98%)
- Shares received: ~98 shares (depends on price)

Total trader cost: 102 SOL (100 bet + 2 fee)
OR: 98 SOL worth of shares for 100 SOL spent
```

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Traders pay 2% fee on every bet
- ✅ 100% of fees go to story creator
- ✅ No platform fees (yet)
- ✅ Creator earns from trading volume (not just initial liquidity)

## Major Prediction Markets Comparison

### 1. **Polymarket** (Largest Crypto Market, $10B+ Volume)
**Fee Structure:**
- **Trading fees:** ~2% spread (goes to liquidity providers, NOT creators)
- **Market creation:** FREE (anyone can create)
- **Creator earnings:** $0 (creators get nothing!)

**Analysis:**
- Liquidity providers earn fees
- Market creators have ZERO financial incentive
- Quality control through curation only
- Sustainable because of high volume

### 2. **Manifold Markets** (Play Money)
**Fee Structure:**
- **Creator fee:** 5% of trading volume
- **Trader fees:** 5% (paid by traders)
- **Market creation:** FREE (uses "Mana" play money)

**Analysis:**
- Higher creator cut than us (5% vs our 2%)
- Play money = less friction
- Incentivizes creators strongly
- BUT: Not real money

### 3. **Kalshi** (US Regulated)
**Fee Structure:**
- **Trading fees:** 3-4% (includes regulatory compliance)
- **Market creation:** Only staff can create
- **Withdrawal fees:** $20 per withdrawal

**Analysis:**
- Higher fees due to regulation
- No creator earnings (centralized)
- Still successful despite high costs
- Limited to US only

### 4. **PredictIt** (US Political Markets)
**Fee Structure:**
- **Trading fees:** 5% on profits
- **Withdrawal fees:** 5%
- **Market creation:** Only staff

**Analysis:**
- Very high fees (10% total after withdraw)
- But still popular for political betting
- Shows people will pay for unique markets

### 5. **Augur** (Decentralized)
**Fee Structure:**
- **Trading fees:** 1% (goes to REP token holders)
- **Market creation:** ~$1-5 in ETH (gas costs)
- **Creator fees:** 1-2% (optional)

**Analysis:**
- Lowest trading fees
- BUT: High gas costs on Ethereum
- Complex UX
- Low adoption

## Should We Change Our Fees?

### Option A: Keep 2% (RECOMMENDED ✅)
```
Pros:
- Industry standard
- Matches Polymarket
- Incentivizes creators
- Simple and competitive
- Already implemented

Cons:
- Not the absolute lowest
- Polymarket creators get 0% (but we give 2%!)
```

### Option B: Reduce to 1%
```
Pros:
- Most competitive trading fees
- More trader-friendly
- Lower than Polymarket

Cons:
- Less creator incentive ($1 per 100 SOL vs $2)
- May not be sustainable
- Difference too small to matter
```

### Option C: Increase to 3%
```
Pros:
- Better creator economics
- Still competitive (matches Kalshi)
- More sustainable

Cons:
- Less competitive than Polymarket
- May discourage small trades
- Not necessary yet
```

### Option D: Add 1% Platform Fee (2% creator + 1% platform = 3% total)
```
Pros:
- Sustainable development funding
- Still competitive
- Creators keep 2%

Cons:
- Higher total fees (3% vs 2%)
- More complex to explain
```

2. **Competitive:** Lower than Kalshi (3-4%), PredictIt (5-10%), Manifold (5%)

4. **Simple:** One fee, easy to understand
5. **Proven:** Works at scale (Polymarket has $10B+ volume with 2%)
6. **Already Implemented:** No code changes needed
